Every theoretical claim in the paper is internally consistent and re-derives correctly. I independently re-derived the debiased-estimator definition and the Theorem 6/8 Gaussian-plus-bias decomposition (reconstruction error ≈ 3e-10), the conditional covariance σ²MΣ̂Mᵀ, Lemma 12's variance lower bound, Theorem 16's power function G (G(α,0)=α and monotonicity), Corollary 10's constant (160=16×10), and the non-Gaussian Lindeberg argument — all verified. On the simulation side the qualitative behavior reproduces: overall and inactive-coordinate coverage come in at ≈0.93–0.95 against the reported ≈0.97, false-positive rates at α=0.05 are near nominal (0.051–0.080 vs 0.038–0.055), and power (true-positive rate) for the proposed method matches well (TP≈1.0 at b=0.5/0.25, 0.87 vs 0.8 at b=0.1). However, without the authors' code/seed, exact figures are not reproducible: active-coordinate coverage (0.58–0.93 vs reported 0.90–1.00) and interval lengths (which vary with b in my run but are near-constant in the paper) deviate — traced to my approximation of the scaled-LASSO σ̂ and to finite-sample bias that is not negligible at s₀=10 (√n/log p ≈ 4.9). The real-data riboflavin results and the competitor columns (R hdi, no data) are not checkable from the article alone. No P0 (nothing is false or impossible); one P1 (no reproduction kit), two P2, one P3.

Why deviations occur (cause), per REPRO_STANDARDS §4: (a) No author seed/code — reimplementation uses a fresh random design realization, so bit-for-bit identity is impossible by construction; (b) σ̂ approximation — strong-signal σ̂ inflates to ≈√(1+θ0ᵀΣθ0) (1.05→1.86 for b=0.1→0.5; true σ=1), which widens CI in the strong regime and makes ℓ vary with b; exact scaled-LASSO σ̂ not recoverable; (c) finite-sample bias — |MΣ̂−I|∞ off-diagonals scale with s₀·µ·b (here s₀·√(logp/n)·b ≈ 10·0.16·0.5 = 0.8 ≫ σ/√n ≈ 0.03), so active-coordinate coverage drops below nominal at s₀=10 where √n/log p ≈ 4.9 — exactly the regime the large-n theory says needs s₀=o(√n/log p).

Findings (for the authors)

P1 — high

JM-001 — No code, data, or RNG seed shipped ⇒ the core numerical magnitudes are not bit-for-bit reproducible and active-coordinate coverage is not confirmed from the article alone. JMLR carries no supplementary code; §1.1's R implementation URL is legacy. An independent implementation (fresh design, Python) reproduces the qualitative picture (overall/inactive coverage ≈0.93–0.95 vs 0.97; FP ≈ nominal; power TP≈1.0/0.87 vs 1.0/0.8) but not exact values: active-coordinate coverage 0.58–0.93 vs reported 0.90–1.00, and interval lengths that depend on b. Primary cause: no seed and an undocumented σ̂ estimator whose strong-signal behavior (σ̂→√(1+θ0ᵀΣθ0)≈1.86 for b=0.5) I could not match; a second, intrinsic cause is finite-sample bias at s₀=10 that the large-n guarantee (s₀=o(√n/log p)) does not yet control. Fix: deposit the sslasso R implementation, a fixed seed, the exact scaled-LASSO σ̂ routine, and the design-generation code (e.g., OSF/Zenodo supplement); report active coverage in a regime satisfying s₀=o(√n/log p).

P2 — medium

JM-002 — Reported interval lengths are near-constant across b (0.176–0.187), but a faithful run shows strong b-dependence (0.110–0.193). ℓ is a load-bearing output (underlies "nearly optimal size"). The b-dependence in my run is driven by my σ̂ approximation, but the paper does not document σ̂ well enough to discriminate a stable protocol from σ̂ sensitivity. Fix: disclose the σ̂ formula/iterations and verify ℓ is b-invariant on the author code; publish representative [MΣ̂Mᵀ]_ii values.

JM-003 — Real-data (riboflavin) and competitor-method (multisample-splitting, ridge projection) exhibits are not reproducible from the article. These require the Bühlmann et al. (2014) data (n=71,p=4088) and the R hdi package; neither the data nor the package is shipped/available, so a third of the numerical exhibits cannot be independently confirmed (➖). Fix: ship the riboflavin data + exact hdi call scripts, or cite a persistent data DOI.

P3 — low

JM-004 — Figures 1–4 embed generated values that were not archived as data. Q-Q and p-value-CDF plots (§5.1, §5.2) are PDF plots with no exported coordinates; their claims are only qualitatively checkable. Fix: export the plotted coordinates or ship the generator script with the article.

What this audit does not guarantee

  • No exact simulation reproduction. Author code/data/seed were unavailable; all Table-1/2 magnitudes are reimplemented on a fresh random design and compared at the qualitative/magnitude level (✅/≈/⚠/➖), not byte-for-byte.
  • The competitor columns and riboflavin results are not independently recomputed (no data, no hdi); marked ➖.
  • The σ̂ (scaled-LASSO) estimator is an approximation; the author's exact routine could not be recovered, and this is the primary source of length/active-coverage deviation (JM-001/002).
  • Asymptotic constants in Theorems 7(a) (ν₀, φ₀, c₁) are order-level and were checked for internal consistency, not numerically verified.
  • Figure content was not raster-diffed; only their statistical content (Gaussianity/uniformity) was reproduced indirectly.
