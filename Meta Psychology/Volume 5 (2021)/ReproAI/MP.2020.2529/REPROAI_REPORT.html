Reproduction detail

Method. The appendix supplies the function simulation() in which p_ointless <- runif(1,0,0.0499) (always < 0.05, hence always "publishable") is compared against a fixed p <- 0.049 (also < 0.05, hence always "publishable"). The result is mean(replicate(10000, simulation())).

Agreement. Exactly 100% congruence (10,000/10,000). The claim "the results of p_ointless and traditional p-hacked results are congruent in 100% of cases" is reproduced exactly.

Interpretation. The result is trivially true: both strategies always yield p < .05, so they always agree. This is the article's satirical point (any procedure that always produces a significant result is "100% effective"). No empirical data is involved; the article is a demonstration/editorial.

Advisory items

  • ADV-001 (P3). The "reproduction" confirms the arithmetic of the simulation, but the simulation is intentionally degenerate (both arms always significant by construction), so this is a satire demonstration rather than a substantive empirical result.
