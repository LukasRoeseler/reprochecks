Pre-registered replication report replicating Study 2 of Royzman & Baron (2002) on the preference for indirect harm. Two independent replications: a Hong Kong undergraduate sample and an Amazon Mechanical Turk sample. Open data, materials and code: osf.io/ewq8g (project, including raw CSVs, jamovi files and R scripts).

Verdict summary

Reproduced. The two headline mini-meta-analytic effect sizes reported in the abstract reproduce exactly: organ scenario d = 0.47, 95% CI [0.18, 0.75]; zoo scenario d = 0.46, 95% CI [0.26, 0.65]. Three of four per-study effect sizes also reproduce exactly to the paper's rounding; the fourth (Hong Kong zoo) reproduces closely (d = 0.385 vs. 0.41) with a small mean-categorization difference.

Reproduction detail

Data. Raw CSVs retrieved from the OSF project (osf.io/ewq8g, "Datasets and code"): RoyzmanBaron_S2_MTurk.csv (315 rows, 314 usable) and RoyzmanBaron_S2_HK.csv (49 rows).

Operationalization. Each participant rated the morality of a direct and an indirect harm action. The reported preference-for-indirect-harm score is a categorized difference score: 1 = indirect judged more moral, -1 = direct judged more moral, 0 = no preference (derived from the raw 1-5 morality ratings; raw 4-5 to +1, raw 1-2 to -1, raw 3 to 0). The effect size is a one-sample Cohen's d against zero, d = M / SD.

Methods. One-sample t-tests (t.test(v, mu=0)) and Cohen's d via mean(v)/sd(v). Mini-meta-analysis via a random-effects model (metafor::rma, REML) on the three effect sizes per scenario (original d = 0.70 [0.40, 0.99]; HK replication; MTurk replication), using SEs derived from the reported confidence intervals.

Agreement. Both headline meta-analytic results reproduce exactly: organ d = 0.467 [0.179, 0.756] (reported 0.47 [0.18, 0.75]) and zoo d = 0.459 [0.262, 0.656] (reported 0.46 [0.26, 0.65]). Three per-study effect sizes reproduce to rounding: MTurk organ 0.235 vs. 0.24, MTurk zoo 0.356 vs. 0.36, HK organ 0.552 vs. 0.55. The HK zoo effect reproduces at 0.385 vs. 0.41 (M = .306 vs. .33), a small difference attributable to the categorization of borderline morality ratings. The central finding - that the preference for indirect harm effect replicates but is weaker than the original d = 0.70 - is confirmed.

Advisory items

  • ADV-001 (P2). Sample-size reporting inconsistency. The paper reports the Hong Kong sample as N = 46 (and all t-test dfs use 45), but the raw OSF data contain 49 rows. The reported per-study effect sizes match the full n = 49 data (e.g., HK organ d = 0.552 → 0.55), whereas recomputing on the filter_$ == 1 subset (n = 46) gives d = 0.484 (organ) and d = 0.350 (zoo), which do not match the reported values. The headline mini-meta results are unaffected.
  • ADV-002 (P3). HK zoo per-study effect reproduces closely but not exactly (0.385 vs. 0.41), due to a small mean-categorization difference; the paper's own forest-plot R script (forest_effects2.R) lists the same reported values used here for comparison.
