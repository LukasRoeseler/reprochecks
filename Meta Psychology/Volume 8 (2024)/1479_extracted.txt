to misrepresent the phenotypic (genetic) architecture of human traits. The impact
of this misrepresentation on the accuracy of h2 estimation is unknown. We aimed to
shed some light on this general issue, by undertaking three simulation studies. In
each, we investigated the parameter recovery performance of five modes- Falconer’s

employed. Theresultsshowedthatthemorecomplicatedthegeneratingarchitecture,
