Conventions (declared up front, REPRO §3)

  • ANOVA error model — SPSS UNIANOVA /METHOD=SSTYPE(3) → replicated as Type III with sum-to-zero (contr.sum) contrasts in R (car::Anova), which matches SPSS for unbalanced designs. Default R treatment contrasts do not match SPSS here; this is why the contrast convention is declared.
  • Residual change scores — regress Time-2 DV on Time-1 DV and take standardized residuals (SPSS SAVE ZRESID). Any constant residual rescaling leaves F invariant, so F does not depend on the exact standardization; recomputed in R and Python.
  • Repeated-measures effect size — Hedges' gav (Lakens 2013) for Studies 3a–3c; gs for between-subjects studies, per Table 1 note. Exact t/RM-F recomputed from raw paired data.
  • Meta-analysis — random-effects via metafor (Viechtbauer 2010) on one simple effect per study (author-stated rationale). Recomputed with REML (primary) and a fixed-effect/DL style cross-check.
  • 0/1 coding direction — SPSS group/condition level order affects only the sign of t; magnitudes and p are invariant and are the basis of the ✅/≈ verdicts (sign orientation noted explicitly where relevant).

Findings (for the authors)

P2 — medium

ADV-001 — Study 2 victim-group main effect does not reproduce to the printed F. Reported F(1,92)=15.74, p<.001, η²p=.17. Independent recompute (R=Python, Type III with SPSS-matching sum-to-zero contrasts on residualized prejudice-change scores) gives F=18.53, p<.001, η²p=.168 — the exact F differs by 2.79 while significance, direction and partial-η² concur. Alternative DV recipes (raw change scores, raw post-test, naive residuals) do not produce 15.74 either. The two hypothesis-critical effects of Study 2 (bogus-pipeline main F=0.046 p=.830 and interaction F=0.010 p=.919) reproduce exactly. Impact: a significant-but-secondary main effect only; no conclusion changes. Fix: re-run the Study 2 residual-change ANOVA on the final dataset and reconcile the published 15.74 with the shipped-data 18.53 (confirm the exact residualization/analysis version).

ADV-002 — Reported participant exclusions are not reflected in shipped-data denominators. Study 4b Method: "recruited 196 … four participants were excluded" (⇒ 192); the shipped raw file contains 200 rows, and after the 4 exclusions the analysed N=196 — matching t(194). Study 5 Method: "two participants dropped out and were deleted" (⇒ 96); the shipped data contain N=98 and the empathy t-test is run on N=98 (t(96)). In both cases the t-tests themselves reproduce exactly on the analysed N, so no statistical conclusion is affected. Fix: align Methods N prose (recruited/excluded) with Table 1 and t-test df.

ADV-003 — Study 3 exclusion-count prose internally inconsistent. "Five other participants were excluded … (six exclusions in Study 3b and four in Study 3c)" — five versus six+four=ten. The shipped phase-2 OSF files contain exactly the reported retained rater Ns (56/43/64), so the retained counts are correct and only the parenthetical is wrong. Fix: correct the parenthetical to match "five" or state per-study exclusions summing to the observed drop.

P3 — low

ADV-004 — Meta-analysis Q/I² differ from values recomputed from Table 1's rounded inputs. Reported Q(7)=27.14, I²=72.26%; recomputed from the printed two-decimal g/SE: Q=26.32 (p=.0004), I²=71.9% (REML) / 73.4% (fixed). Gap fully explained by rounding the published effect sizes to two decimals; headline (≈72% heterogeneity, pooled ≈0) unchanged. Fix: optional — report from unrounded inputs for exactness.

ADV-005 — Study 5 donation t magnitude/sign orientation. Reported t(46)=0.74, g=−0.21, with Holocaust M=18.48 < control M=21.52. Recomputed |t|=0.735 (p=.466); g recomputes to −0.208. The reported positive t is the reverse order (Control−Holocaust) of the negative g; an orientation choice. No conclusion changes. Fix: optional — report t(46)=−0.73 to match the negative g under a single group-order convention.

What this package already does right

  • Genuine open-science posture that is substantiated on disk: full raw per-item datasets, score-computation + analysis SPSS syntax, and materials for all 8 studies are on OSF (via linked project ja3yx); a third-party analysis reproduction (Mühlmeister) is documented in the editorial record.
  • The headline empirical claim — no experimental evidence for secondary antisemitism, with a meta-analytic pooled effect indistinguishable from zero under substantial heterogeneity — reproduces from raw data in two independent languages.
  • Nearly every reported test statistic (ANOVA Fs, t-tests, correlations, stabilities, Table-1 paired tests with exact M/SD) reproduces to the printed precision; effect-size conventions are explicitly stated (Hedges' gs vs gav, Lakens 2013).
  • Honest "file-drawer" framing: null results are reported plainly rather than over-interpreted.

What this audit does not guarantee

  • No SPSS live execution. SPSS GUI-only was not batch-automatable on this host; the author .sps syntax was read and its intended analyses re-implemented in R and Python from the shipped CSVs (two-language agreement). A genuine SPSS re-run against the .sav files is offered as optional cross-validation.
  • ADV-001 (Study 2 victim main) not reconciled to 15.74: several DV recipes were tried; none yields the printed F; the specific original residualization/analysis version is not recoverable from the shipped files without re-running an older SPSS dataset version. The reproduction value 18.53 (p<.001, η²p=.168) is reported honestly as the independent result.
  • Secondary analyses not re-run: moderator hierarchical regressions (supplementary tables Osm.1–7), Study 3 explicit-warmth/likability ANOVAs, individual-clasification-image likability ANOVA, reverse-correlation trial filtering, and single-rater word-stem coding were recorded but not recomputed end-to-end (several require the non-de-identified individual-level files); they are marked in the inventory where applicable.
  • Cited background numbers (e.g., original-study f=0.36, the primary/secondary antisemitism latent r=.84 from Imhoff 2010, power analyses) are contextual and were not recomputed here.
  • Demographic/attention-check prose (age/gender compositions, 45%/27% attention-check failure rates, reliabilities) was recorded but not independently re-derived from the raw files beyond sample-count confirmation.
