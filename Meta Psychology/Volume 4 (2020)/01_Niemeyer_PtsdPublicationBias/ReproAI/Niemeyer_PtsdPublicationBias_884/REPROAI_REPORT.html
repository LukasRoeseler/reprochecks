P2 — medium (4)

ADV-002 — Selection-model non-convergence: text says two, Appendix A shows three; the convergence set differs by analysis unit.
Evidence: paper_extracted.txt line 613 "the selection model approach did not converge for two data sets"; Appendix A prints "No con-/vergence" for IDs 70 (line 1288), 80 (line 1474), 44 (line 1547) — three cells. My re-run reproduces Appendix A's first-loop (res8_sel.csv): NA at IDs 44, 70, 80 (3); the d-scale loop used for Table 1 (res9_d.csv): NA at IDs 15, 18 (2). The two loops use different units (original vs Cohen's-d transformed) and different step cut-point logic.
Why it matters: Prose-vs-table inconsistency and an ambiguous count; Table 1's selection-model row (d-loop) is nonetheless internally correct and reproduces.
Fix: State which data sets and which analysis unit each "did not converge" claim refers to; make Appendix A and prose agree.

ADV-003 — Figure 1 flowchart arithmetic is off by ~5.
Evidence: data-set exclusion categories sum to 2,040 not 2,017; 2,017 (excluded) + 98 (included) = 2,115 ≠ 2,110 (total); "95.4%" equals 2012/2110 (=2,110−98) but not 2017/2110 (=95.6%).
Why it matters: The central screening-flowchart accounting in the "why we kept only 4.6%" narrative is internally inconsistent.
Fix: Reconcile category counts to sum to 2,017 (or correct the total) and align the % with the adopted count.

ADV-004 — "obtained data from six authors (16.7%)" denominator is ambiguous.
Evidence: 6/36 contacted = 16.7%; 6/83 meta-analyses = 7.2%. Paired with "contacted 36 authors (43.4%)", 16.7% reads as 6/36 but is unexplained.
Why it matters: Misleading percentage in the recruitment narrative.
Fix: "six of the 36 contacted authors (16.7%)" or "six of 83 meta-analyses (7.2%)".

ADV-005 — Impossible CI and sign flip in the ID=44 illustrative example.
Evidence: Text "PET-PEESE (0.44, 95% CI [−1.079, −1.958])" — lower > upper. Re-run: 0.44, CI [−1.079, 1.958]. Text "traditional meta-analysis (−0.153, 95% CI [−0.084, 0.39])" vs Appendix A / re-run +0.153 [−0.084, 0.39].
Why it matters: Illustrative/figure example only; no primary inference changes, but a reader cannot parse the CI and the sign is confusing.
Fix: Correct CI to [−1.079, 1.958]; reconcile the MA sign with Appendix A.

P3 — low (2)

ADV-006 — The 12 Hedges'-g data sets are not enumerated. The gs.ds.lors mapping in Code_Analyses_R3.R (lines 668–669) silently decides which data sets keep g instead of d; the paper neither lists them nor quantifies the g-vs-d magnitude. Fix: enumerate the 12 IDs and report the difference.

ADV-007 — Shipped code is not self-contained. Code_Analyses_R3.R / sim_power.R hard-code setwd("C:/Dropbox/..."), source(".../res_sim.txt"), load(".../res2_*.RData") and Dropbox write.csv2 paths; the XLConnect loader requires Java (unavailable here, hence the documented openxlsx swap). The analytic core runs and reproduces Table 1 after a loader swap, but out-of-the-box portability is impaired. Fix: bundle res_sim.txt/res2_*.RData (or regenerate), remove absolute paths, use a Java-free xlsx reader.

What this submission already does right

  • Genuine open data + open reproducible analysis: raw effect-size data, full analysis code and simulation code are live on OSF and were re-run successfully; the Open-Data/Open-analysis badges are warranted.
  • Headline analysis reproduces exactly: all of Table 1 and every reported difference score match the re-run at full precision — not just "close".
  • Comprehensive pre-analysis conventions declared: FE/DL model choice, p-uniform side/alpha/zeroing rule (α/4), PET-vs-PEESE α=0.1 rule, selection-model step logic are all in the code/paper.
  • Honest interpretation given low power: the authors refrain from over-interpreting the low-powered bias tests and report correction-method results transparently.

What this audit does not guarantee

  • Byte-level Monte-Carlo reproduction: the full 10,000-iteration simulation was not re-run end-to-end (prohibitive compute on this host). Simulation claims were verified against the author's shipped full-run res2_*.csv, not an independent full rerun; the reduced-scale independent Python run confirms logic but not exact Figure 3 ordinates.
  • Multi-core RNG reproducibility of the sim: the author's sim_power.R uses set.seed(9419*pos) but clusterApplyLB (load balancing) makes the exact draw sequence run-dependent; no claim of byte-identical Figure 3 is made.
  • Inclusion/eligibility coding: the claim inventory's flowchart counts (C1–C9) reflect author decisions; individual study-level screening and the "12 Hedges'-g" list were not independently re-coded from primary literature.
  • External citations (C50–C52): background numbers (Fanelli; Kessler; Driessen) were not re-derived.
  • Sign conventions in illustrative examples were not adjudicated against the primary studies.
