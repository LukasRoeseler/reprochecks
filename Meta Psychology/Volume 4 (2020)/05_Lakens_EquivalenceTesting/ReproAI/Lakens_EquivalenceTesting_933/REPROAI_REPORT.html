Findings (for the authors)

P2 — medium

ADV-001 — Prose typo in the third TOST/SGPV correspondence sentence. The manuscript states: "…we focus on their correspondence at three values, namely where the TOST p = 0.025 and SGPV is 1, where the TOST p = 0.5 and SGPV = 0.5, and where the TOST p = 0.975 and SGPV = 1." The third item should read SGPV = 0 (equivalently 1−SGPV = 1): at TOST p = 0.975 the 95% CI falls outside the equivalence range and the data favour the alternative hypothesis. The correction is confirmed by our recomputation — at the corresponding mean (≈147.75) TOST p = 0.9750 and SGPV = 0.0000 — and by the paper's own Figure 2, Figure 1 caption ("1−SGPV = 1"), and the "situation C" paragraph ("the p-value will always be 0.975, and the SGPV is 0"). The error is a genuine published typo: it appears in the author's own reproducible source (manuscript.R2.Rmd line 136), not merely in our PDF extraction. Impact: cosmetic prose error; no numeric or inferential consequence (the governing figures and the next sentence are correct). Occurred in the extracted text at paper_extracted.txt lines 96–98.
Fix: change "and SGPV = 1" to "and SGPV = 0" (or "and 1-SGPV = 1") in that sentence.

P3 — low

ADV-002 — Published OSF links point to empty projects; all open materials actually live on GitHub. The badge line "All supplementary files can be accessed at OSF: ZP3KF" and the Authors' Note URL osf.io/8crkg both resolve to OSF projects whose osfstorage contained 0 files on 2026-09-14 (OSF API totals = 0). The complete reproducible package — manuscript.R2.Rmd, functions/SGPV_function.R, p_tost_converter*.R, TOST_to_SGPV.R, the TOST_vs_SGPV_app/ Shiny app source, and an archive/ with the derivation scripts — exists at the GitHub repository github.com/Lakens/TOST_vs_SGPV that the Authors' Note explicitly cites. The Open Materials / Open & reproducible analysis badges are therefore substantiated, and this re-audit reproduced every claim from that repository — but the specific URLs a reader is told to click are empty. Impact: findability/archival hygiene; broken-link risk; contradicts "all supplementary files at OSF ZP3KF".
Fix: upload the functions, reproducible .Rmd, Shiny app source and figures to ZP3KF (and/or 8crkg), or reword the badge line to point to GitHub.

What this audit does not guarantee

  • No live re-knit of the author's manuscript.Rmd. We sourced and ran the author's p_delta() function directly and reimplemented TOST/SGPV from first principles, but we did not execute the full manuscript.Rmd (it requires the CRAN TOSTER package and knitr on the audit host, which was not configured). The closed-form outputs are byte-equivalent to the author's function, so this gap is non-blocking.
  • Figures 2, 3, 5, 7, 10 and 11 are 1−SGPV / parametric-transform plots of the verified quantities; we verified their parent statistics but did not pixel-diff raster PDF renders (not meaningful, and the underlying values all reproduce).
  • Cited background values from Blume et al. (2018) (n=4: 97.60% and ~36%) are quoted in-text; we reproduced them to ≈ and independently simulated the sampling statistic, but their exact published precision depends on the original authors' CI-truncation/seeding convention.
  • Qualitative/directional claims (e.g. "multiple comparisons are not obviated", the Conclusion strengths/weaknesses) were checked for internal consistency, not formally tested — they have no independent numeric source to recompute against.
