Abstract
We have detected coding errors in the meta-analysis of Hofmann et al. (2015) who investigated the effect of in-
tranasal oxytocin on psychiatric symptoms. We demonstrate that, after correcting these errors and reanalysing the
data,themainconclusionsofHofmannetal.(2015)arenolongersupported.
Keywords: meta-analysis,codingerrors,intranasaloxytocin
