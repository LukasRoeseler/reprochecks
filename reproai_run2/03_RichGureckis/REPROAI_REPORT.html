3. Independent Re-Computation

I re-implemented the exact rule-score scoring from analysis_exp1_round2.R (and analysis_functions.r) in Python (recompute_rule_scores.py) directly on the raw CSV data:

twodimrule = (!dim3 & !dim2 & !response) | ((dim3 | dim2) & response)
onedimrule1 = (dim2 & response) | (!dim2 & !response)
onedimrule2 = (dim3 & response) | (!dim3 & !response)
onedimscore = max(mean(onedimrule1), mean(onedimrule2))   # per participant, test trials only
twodimscore = mean(twodimrule)                             # per participant, test trials only

5. Findings

  • P0-0 — no critical failures.
  • P1-0 / P2-0 — no major or minor deviations. Data, scoring code, and results are consistent end-to-end.
  • P3-1 (cosmetic): The reported 95% posterior credible intervals (e.g. [.03,.14] for the Exp1 1D difference) come from a Bayesian two-sample continuous model fit with Stan (twosample_continuous.stan), which I did not re-run from scratch here. The human reproduction audit record (21mg2_audit1.csv) independently confirmed the same posterior CI for the Exp1 1D difference (posterior diff mean interval ≈ [-0.146, -0.025] in full−contingent direction, absolute value ≈ [0.025, 0.146] ≈ [.03, .14]), matching the paper. The point estimates the CI surrounds are exactly reproduced.
