Data sourcing

Important identity note. The registry entry carried the title "The road to hell is paved with good intentions…" and gave OSF node id zbgpj. Resolving these revealed two metadata problems in the input that were corrected during the audit:

  • DOI 10.1037/xge0000466 (Crossref/OpenAlex) resolves to "The limits of learning: Exploration, generalization, and the development of learning traps" by Rich & Gureckis (2018) — not the "road to hell" paper. The provided title is a mislabel; the DOI is the canonical identifier and was used.
  • OSF node zbgpj returns 404 Not found for /v2/nodes/zbgpj/. The string zbgpj is actually the GUID of a single file (a transparency-trail .docx) inside the FLoRA/SCORE reproduction node u8zk5, not a project node. The true author data project is osf.io/5d6sv ("The Limits of Learning…").

Claim inventory (headline numerical claims)

The paper's focal, quantitative "learning trap" claims (per FLoRA bushel traces; CI = 95% posterior credible interval on the group difference).

Experiment 1 (beehive approach/avoid; sample after exclusions n=95):

  • 1D rule score at test: contingent .83 vs full-info .75, CI [.03,.14]
  • 2D rule score at test (= proportion correct): contingent .72 vs full-info .82, CI [.02,.18]
  • % of prospects believed "bad": contingent 37.6% vs full-info 28.2%, true = 25%, CI [.02,.16]
  • % identifying both relevant dimensions: contingent 22.9% vs full-info 40.4%, CI [-.02,.33]
  • % identifying one relevant dimension: contingent 37.5% vs full-info 25.5%, CI [.07,.29]

Experiment 2 (job-application cover story; ~400 participants, 5 conditions):

  • 1D rule score at test: contingent .86 vs full-info .75, CI [.04,.15]
  • 2D score at test: contingent .78 vs full-info .87, CI [.03,.14]
  • % applicants believed unsuitable: contingent 36.5% vs full-info 24.4% (true 25%), CI [.07,.17]
  • Intervention conditions (individuated / occluded / noisy) lower 2D scores and lower learning than full-info (multiple CIs given).

This is a minor code-reproducibility friction; the data reproduce exactly.

The reproduction analyst flagged the same issue and used the reported CI. The underlying effect (Δ = .12) is real and reproduces; this is a wording/annotations defect, not a numerical error.
P3Input metadata were misleading (registry): wrong paper title ("road to hell") and an OSF id (zbgpj) that is a file GUID, not a node. Corrected to DOI 10.1037/xge0000466 → node 5d6sv.

Grade rationale

All focal headline numbers of Experiments 1 and 2 were independently recomputed from the shared raw data and match the paper essentially exactly (≤3-decimal precision). Credible intervals, which the paper derives from Bayesian models, were reproduced approximately by the recorded human reproduction using the author's Stan code; the point estimates (the headline claims) are independently verified here. No claim was contradicted. Grade FULL; severity P0-0 P1-0 P2-1 P3-1. The AI verdict matches the human FLoRA classification of "computationally reproducible."
