# Data Key

## Question data

**antennae** [Exp 1]: Whether the participant answered that the antennae were relevant to bee danger.

**body** [Exp 1]: Whether the participant answered that the body was relevant to bee danger.

**changingstrategy**: Free text response indicating if participant's strategy changed.

**completelearn** [Exp S2, 2]: Whether the participant reported learning the task completely ("yes"), 
possibly missed some aspects ("mid"), or definitely missed some aspects ("no").

**condition**: Participant condition (contingent or full_info)

**counterbalance**: Participant counterbalance (0 to 5). Determines mapping of abstract category structure to visible dimensions.

**dangerpercent**: Percent of stimuli participant believed to be negative.

**degree** [Exp S1, S2, 2]: Whether the participant answered that the degrees was relevant to applicant suitability.

**difficulty**: Participant's rating of task difficulty, on 0 to 10 scale.

**education**: Participant's self-reported education level.

**engagement**: Participant's rating of task engagement, on 0 to 10 scale.

**finalscore**: Cash bonus achieved by participant.

**gender**: Participant's self-reported gender.

**howtoimprove**: Participant's free text feedback on how to improve the task.

**instructionloops**: Number of times the participant failed the post-instructions quiz.

**legs** [Exp 1]: Whether the participant answered that the legs were relevant to bee danger.

**major**: Participant's self-reported college major.

**numdimchecked**: Number of dimensions participant selected as relevant to stimulus value.

**pastemployer** [Exp S1, S2, 2]: Whether the participant answered that the past employer was relevant to applicant suitability.

**pastposition** [Exp S1, S2, 2]: Whether the participant answered that the past position was relevant to applicant suitability.

**penpaper**: Whether the participant self-reported using pen and paper to aid in the task.

**skill**: [Exp S1, S2, 2]: Whether the participant answered that the applicant's skill was relevant to applicant suitability.

**strategy**: Free text response indicating the participant's strategy during the task.

**uniqueid**: Unique identifying number for the participant.

**wings** [Exp 1]: Whether the participant answered that the wings were relevant to bee danger.

**exclude**: Indicates whether participant was excluded from analyses.

**rightdimensions**: Indicates whether participants selected the correct two dimensions as relevant to stimulus value.

**trapdimensions**: Indicates whether participants selected just one of the correct two dimensions as relevant to stimulus value.

## Task data

**ap_payoff**: Payoff for approaching the stimulus on the current trial.

**av_payoff**: Payoff for avoiding the stimulus on the current trial.

**block**: Block number (each block contains 16 trials).

**category**: Wether the stimulus is in the good (1) or bad (0) category (assuming no outcome noise).

**category_withnoise** [Exp S2, 2]: Wether the stimulus on the current trial will actually produce a good or bad outcome given outcome noise.

**condition**: Participant condition (contingent or full_info)

**correct**: Whether participant selected the optimal response.

**counterbalance**: Participant counterbalance (0 to 5). Determines mapping of abstract category structure to visible dimensions.

**dim0**: Value on dimension 0 in underlying abstract category structure (always irrelevant).

**dim1**: Value on dimension 1 in underlying abstract category structure (always irrelevant).

**dim2**: Value on dimension 2 in underlying abstract category structure (always relevant, 1 is good).

**dim3**: Value on dimension 3 in underlying abstract category structure (always relevant, 1 is good).

**fullinfo**: Whether participant received full feedback regardless of choice on the current trial.

**imagenum**: Id of visual stimulus (0-15) shown to participant on the current trial.

**indiv_value** [Exp S1, S2, 2]: Id of individuation value for current stimulus (-1 when individuation not used).

**physdim0**: Visual dimension mapped to abstract dimension 0.

**physdim1**: Visual dimension mapped to abstract dimension 1.

**physdim2**: Visual dimension mapped to abstract dimension 2.

**physdim3**: Visual dimension mapped to abstract dimension 3.

**obscure** [Exp S1, S2, 2]: Whether a dimension was occluded on the current trial.

**obscureNum** [Exp S1, S2, 2]: Which abstract dimension was occluded on the current trial (-1 when no dimension was occluded).

**obscureNumPhys** [Exp S1, S2, 2]: Which physical dimension was occluded on the current trial (-1 when no dimension was occluded).

**response**: Participant's response (1 for approach/harvest, 0 for avoid).

**reward**: Participant's received reward on the current trial.

**rt** [Exp S1, S2, 2]: Reaction time on current trial.

**score_post**: Participant's total score following the trial.

**score_pre**: Participant's total score at the start of the trial.

**stimnum**: Id of stimulus in abstract category structure (0-15).

**swapdim0**: Whether 0/1 values are swapped in the the physical dimension mapped to abstract dimension 0.

**swapdim1**: Whether 0/1 values are swapped in the the physical dimension mapped to abstract dimension 1.

**swapdim2**: Whether 0/1 values are swapped in the the physical dimension mapped to abstract dimension 2.

**swapdim3**: Whether 0/1 values are swapped in the the physical dimension mapped to abstract dimension 3.

**test**: Whether the participant is in the test phase.

**trial**: Trial number of current trial.

**uniqueid**: Unique identifying number for the participant.

**exclude**: Indicates whether participant was excluded from analyses.
