Human Benchmark (from open metadata, not from the gated report text)

From the RWI/I4R/IDEAS/OSF abstracts (retrievable):

  • Dupraz checked that ABH results are fully reproducible using their data and code.
  • Claims are robust to (1) narrowing the age window for building the linked dataset, (2) modifying the specification, (3) computing different standard errors.
  • Only significant discrepancy: famine effects are stronger under a narrower linking-age window, plausibly because it reduces measurement error.
  • OSF metadata records an authors’ response signing off that “everything replicated.”
