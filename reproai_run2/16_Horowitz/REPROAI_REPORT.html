4. Key Analytical Finding

The FLoRA reproduction’s focal interaction is POSITIVE (degree_proportion × BA Degree = +0.088, t=2.316, p=0.019), which is the opposite sign of the paper’s headline claim (-1.648, negative, p<0.001). The reproduction’s own horowitz_final_report.pdf states verbatim: “This fails to replicate the original result.” The later, independent FLoRA audit (2023–24) re-ran the reproduction code and likewise obtained a positive focal coefficient (+0.190, t=4.741) — positive again, differing in magnitude from the reproduction’s +0.088 but not reproducing the paper’s negative value in either attempt.

The critical analytical fact is established from the primary documents: the FLoRA reproduction produced an opposite-sign focal interaction (+0.088) to the paper’s headline claim (-1.648), and itself concluded the result was not replicated.

This is consistent with the human FLoRA code flag (“computational issues”): the reproduction — and even the reproduction vs. its own audit re-run — could not produce stable, matching positive/negative coefficients, and computational choices (CFA factor scoring, occupation-code harmonization, sample construction) are implicated.
