Verdict vs human "computationally reproducible"

The FLoRA registry records one reproduction (Gereke et al. 2019, "without author data"); the recorded outcome is no outcome recorded in the atlas, but the authored SCORE report documents a successful reproduction: the focal "Proportion natives" coefficient was reproduced as -0.47 (SE 0.07) against the paper's -0.51 (SE 0.06), statistically significant and same-sign across all six robustness specifications. The substantive conclusion (more natives ⇒ less ethnic homophily) consistently reproduces in the human attempt.

My automated verdict here is NODATA for independent recomputation, because of the gated dataset. This is an environmental/data-access limit, not evidence against the paper: the human reproduction's numbers support the headline claim, and the paper's core directional result is robust across the reproduction's robustness checks despite the disclosed code bugs.
