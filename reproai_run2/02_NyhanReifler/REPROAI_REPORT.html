2. Claim inventory (headline numerical claims)

The experiment (CCAP/YouGov 2012) randomises respondents to four conditions (control / innuendo / innuendo+denial / innuendo+denial+causal) about a fictional politician, Sen. Don Swensen. Three outcomes: favorability (6-pt), belief he took bribes/illegal acts (5-pt), belief he resigned due to bribery investigation (4-pt). 3. Independent recomputation

Independent pipeline written fresh in Python (statsmodels WLS) for the IPW models and R (survey package, svydesign(id=~1, weights=~weight)) for the survey-weighted models. Weights identified empirically: IPW models use aw; survey models use weight (weights-only design, no strata/PSU), which reproduces survey SEs exactly. All numbers below are computed, not taken from the paper.
