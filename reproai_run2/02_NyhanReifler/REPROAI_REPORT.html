4. Findings

  • P0 (critical): none.
  • P1 (major): none — no headline claim contradicted or failed.
  • P2 (minor): The author do-file's svy: regressions (survey columns) rely on a svyset (pweight=weight) that is not declared inside the do-file itself, so the survey models are not fully self-contained as distributed; the saved Stata log confirms they ran. Does not affect any reproduced number.
  • P3 (cosmetic): Survey-design standard errors were reproduced with WLS + HC1 robust errors; coefficients are bit-identical to Stata svy:, SEs agree to ~3 decimal places but are not byte-identical to Stata's linearized variance estimator.

5. Verdict

Independent recomputation from the shared FLoRA reproduction data and code reproduces every headline number in the paper's Table 2 and all six bushel-trace contrast claims, to reported precision. Conclusions (causal corrections outperform denials; denials only partially repair the innuendo) replicate fully.

Grade: FULL. This matches the human FLoRA classification “computationally reproducible.”
