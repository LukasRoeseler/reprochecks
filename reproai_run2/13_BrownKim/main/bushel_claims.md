This is the single-trace claim selected from this paper in Phase 1. It
includes two levels of claim 3: 3a, a statement of the finding, and 3b,
the specification of the test. In bushel-trace claims, claim 3b is not
coded, and claim 3 is analagous to the single-trace 3a.

-   **Claim 2 (Abstract statement):** Regression results show that types
    predicted by the Epstein–Zin model to prefer early resolution choose
    early resolution with 20%–50% higher probability.

-   **Claim 3a (Finding statement):** Each subject that has
    risk-preference parameters less than
    intertemporal-substitution-preference parameters (alpha &lt; phi)
    should prefer early resolution of uncertainty; they should choose to
    roll immediately in the uncertainty-resolution-preference
    elicitation task.

-   **Claim 3b (Test specification):** We use regression analysis to
    properly test our model | We use regression analysis to properly
    test our model, and we control for the error-in-variables problem
    (the fact that our regressors are estimated quantities) using the
    Erickson-Whited higher-order-moments method.

-   **Claim 4 (Results statement):** Subjects completely classified as
    early-preferring types chose the early option with 50% more
    probability (two-tailed p value &lt; 0.01) than those classified as
    indifferent types. From Table 5 - coefficient of subject choice on
    the posterior probability of being in an early-preferring group =
    0.507, significant at the 1% level.
