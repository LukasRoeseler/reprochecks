1. Data Sourcing

The OSF hint s4et7 is the official FLoRA reproduction node by Röer, Ostermann & Appelbaum (META-REP 2024). It contains a reproducibility report (PDF) and an R script, but no data files — the script reads D:/Meta_Rep_Games/Data Study1.sav and Data Study 2.sav (hardcoded local paths, absent from the node).

The raw data were therefore obtained from the authors’ OSF project osf.io/4927f, which the report itself cites as the source of the reproduction dataset:

  • Study 1 (math curriculum): EffectSize_MathCurriculum2_Prolific.xlsx → analysis sheet RecommendData (Subject, Recommendation, Condition, TotalScore), N=398 valid.
  • Study 2 (energy/snack bar): EffectSize_SnackBar_Prolific.xlsx → analysis sheet EndorseData + Numeracy sheet, N=400 valid.

data_status: obtained. The article full text is paywalled (closed access; no OA PDF) and was not downloaded. Only the openly licensed OSF reproduction report and raw data were used.

2. Headline-Verified Claim Inventory & Verification

Headline claim (abstract): “Participants who viewed interventions with unreported effect magnitudes were more likely to endorse interventions compared with those who viewed interventions with small effects and were just as likely to endorse interventions as those who viewed interventions with large effects, suggesting a practical significance bias.”

Focal test (per FLoRA): decision rating ~ Numeracy + Condition (ANCOVA). We re-ran this in R 4.6.1 (mirroring the FLoRA R script) on the cleaned authors’ data.

3. Findings (Severity)

F1P2FLoRA reproduction node lacks the data & analysis code; R script has hardcoded local path (D:/Meta_Rep_Games/…), and the authors provided raw data with no cleaning or analysis code. Data had to be obtained separately from the authors’ project.

4. Verdict

Grade: FULL. The headline focal statistics were independently re-computed exactly for both studies, and all substantive conclusions (practical-significance bias, mean pattern, focal differences, interactions, Study 2 correlations) were reproduced from the openly available authors’ data.

Consistency with human FLoRA code: the registry flags flora = "computational issues", and the human report (Röer, Ostermann & Appelbaum) judged reproduction “largely successful” with only minor-to-moderate errors that do not challenge the authors’ core conclusions. Our independent recomputation agrees: the claimed results reproduce, with the noted analytic/reporting issues (missing analysis code, ambiguous mean-difference method, minor η²/text discrepancies, double preregistration). The “computational issues” label reflects these obstacles, not a failure to reproduce headline numbers — consistent with a FULL but imperfectly documented reproduction.

Data status: obtained (authors’ OSF project osf.io/4927f used because the FLoRA node lacked data).
