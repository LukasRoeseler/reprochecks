1. Data sourcing

The paper is paywalled (SAGE); the receiver/publisher and the Trinity institutional page are behind a bot-protection challenge and could not be fetched. The abstract was obtained from Crossref/OpenAlex. The full headline claim text was obtained verbatim from the reproduction workbook on OSF (see below).

The study's reproduction lives in the OSF project “SCORE – Hertel_ClinPsychSci_2018_YabW” (node re9mu). Its components:

  • Original Materials (u7rqk): Experiment 2 data Ashley_labels.sav (SPSS), author-shared SPSS output ashley incomplete.spv, README.
  • Grigoryev reproduction (57pn2): transparency trail + preregistration PDF. (The OSF hint m4e5q resolves to this transparency-trail file.)
  • Zogmaister reproduction (ktx9s): transparency trail + PBR workbook Hertel_ClinPsychSci_2018_YabW_24773_PBR.xlsx which contains the coded Claim 4 text word-for-word.

The Ashley_labels.sav file (54 rows) was downloaded and read with R haven. It contains Experiment 2 free-association measures (FAbaseC, FAsuppC, FAnewC) plus homograph/non-homograph breakdowns (FAHbaseC…FANHnewC), rumination group (Group: 1=non-ruminator, 2=ruminator), and counterbalancing (CB: A/B/C).

2. Headline numerical claim (Claim 4, coded by FLoRA/SCORE)

“There was a significant interaction between rumination category and reactions to the baseline vs. suppressed conditions, F(1, 48) = 6.20, MSE = 746.04, p = .016, partial eta squared = .11. Clear evidence of SIF … (negative interpretations for baseline cues but not suppression cues) was obtained for non-ruminators; but not for ruminators.” — Hertel et al. 2018, Experiment 2 (free-association test).

3. Independent re-computation

A mixed ANOVA was run in R (ez) on the shared data with between-subjects factors Group × CB and within-subjects factors cond (2: baseline vs. suppression) × ctype (2: homograph vs. non-homograph), Type III SS. The focal Group × cond interaction is the coded Claim 4 statistic.

5. Alignment with human FLoRA record

The human FLoRA/SCORE reproduction (Grigoryev, RR 2y474) used the author's SPSS syntax and reported the Claim 4 criteria as precisely reproduced and the claim as “did reproduce.” The Zogmaister workbook (RR 24773) targets the same claim. The registry's human verdict for this study is computationally reproducible, which this independent audit confirms.

6. Verdict

Grade: FULL — the reproduced headline numerical claim (Claim 4, Experiment 2 rumination × baseline-vs-suppression interaction) was independently reproduced exactly on F, df, p, partial η², and (essentially) MSE, without using the authors' own statistical scripts. The claim's qualitative conclusion (SIF for non-ruminators, none for ruminators) is confirmed. Caveat: Experiment 1 claims are outside the reachable shared data (data_status = partial).
