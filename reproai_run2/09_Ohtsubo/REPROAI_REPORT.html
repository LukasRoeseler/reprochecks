3. Independent recomputation (focal claim, C4)

I re-ran the analysis from the raw author data in R 4.6.1, independently reconstructing the pipeline: read Sheet3 (skip=1) of the .xls, reverse-recode item r4 (1↔7), compute the 4-item intimacy scale (r1+r4rec+r6+r7)/4, and run a Student independent-samples t-test of Intimacy ~ cnd. The reconstructed scale matched the dataset’s Intimacy column with zero mismatches.

Excluded-participant resolution. The raw data contain 30 subjects (11 male, cnd 1: n=15, cnd 2: n=15), but the paper reports N=29 with 10 males; one male must be dropped. Following the FLoRA analysis, five candidate rows (3, 12, 13, 22, 25) were tested. Only excluding row 22 or 25 reproduces the article; both give identical statistics.

4. Secondary / supplemental checks

Internal-consistency checks on paper-reported summary statistics (means/SDs present in the article), computed independently in R/Python:

  • Study 2a attention–midpoint t ≈ 2.74 (reported 2.77, df 14); no-attn–midpoint t ≈ 5.59 (reported 5.60, df 13).
  • Study 2b intimacy t ≈ 4.11 (reported 4.10), d ≈ 1.25 (reported 1.27); interest-in-friendship t ≈ 1.94 (reported 1.96), d ≈ 0.59 (reported 0.59).

These confirm the reported tests are arithmetically consistent with the reported group means/SDs, but are not raw-data re-runs (Study 2b/2c and Study 1 data were not shared in the reproduction).

F4P3–OSF hint mislabeled. Registry OSF id ga35z is a file GUID, not a project node; the real node is y47pr.

6. Verdict

Grade: FULL for the focal headline claim — the canonical Study 2a statistic (t(27)=5.91, p<.001, d=2.20, N=29, 4.58±.82 vs 2.82±.79) was independently re-computed from the shared raw author data and matches the article exactly at the reported precision.

Headline verified: YES (focal claim). data_status: partial (Study-2a raw data only; rest of paper not shareable for re-run).

Consistency with human FLoRA verdict: The human FLoRA classification is “computationally reproducible”. My independent verdict confirms this for the focal claim, with the caveats in F1–F3 that the reproduction is claim-level and involves a small excluded-participant judgment call that does not change the outcome.

7. Honesty / limitations statement

All numbers in this report were obtained by actually downloading the reproduction files and recomputing them (R 4.6.1, Python 3.8). No values were estimated or fabricated. Claims that could not be re-run from shared data are explicitly labelled as not data-verified (Studies 1a/1b, 2b/2c). The article PDF and its full-text extraction were deleted from this folder after analysis per protocol (copyright); only open reproduction materials, raw data, the analysis script, and this audit’s own scripts/outputs are retained.
