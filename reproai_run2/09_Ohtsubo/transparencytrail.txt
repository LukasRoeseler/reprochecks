Analysis attempt 1
The claim contains variable of “intimacy” which should be calculated according to the article as sum of 4 items (explained on p. 239, “Intimacy was assessed by…”). This does not correspond with the data, which contains 10 items under the common column called Intimacy questionnaire and 1 item called Intimacy. As the first step, I had to figure out, which 4 items belong to the Intimacy scale and whether the Intimacy variable in the dataset is calculated based on 10 or 4 items. After the translation of all items from Japanese language to English I have identified the 4 items, which should form the Intimacy variable (contained in the claim). After calculating the sum score I have compared the scores from the Intimacy item already provided in the data and Intimacy variable calculated from 4 items identified by me. The items had the same values.
The article contains only brief information about excluding 1 participant: &quot;after excluding one participant who suspected the use of deceptive procedures&quot; (p. 241). There is no further information, nor in the data, which specific participant was excluded. Therefore my second step was to find out, which one it was. The method section contains the following information: &quot;Participants were 29 Japanese undergraduates (10 males and 19 females&quot;, p. 241).  The original dataset contains information from 11 males and 19 females. This means that one excluded participant should have male gender. Based on the information about the mean and standard deviation of age reported in the article (&quot;10 males and 19 females, Mage ± SD = 19.3 ± 0.89&quot;, p. 241) the one excluded participant should be case n. 3, 12, 13, 22, or 25. Only when excluding one of these participants does mean and SD of age match with the information in the article.
Based on the original article information provided on p. 241: “Consistent with the hypothesis, intimacy was higher in the attention condition (4.58 ± .82) than in the no attention condition (2.82 ± .79), t27 = 5.91, p = .001, d = 2.20” I have chosen Student t-test for the analysis. Since the article does not mention anything related to equality of variances or Welch t-test, I assumed that the authors have used exactly the Student t-test. P-value is from two-tailed test.
Completed claim: single-trace
Analysis attempts 2-5
Since it was still unclear to me, which one specific participant was excluded, I had to repeat the analysis (Student t-test) 5 times  (each time with the exclusion of different participant, specifically participants n. 3, 12, 13, 22, and 25) and compare the results of t-test with the results reported in the article for a possible match. The data with the exclusion of participants 22 and 25 produced identical results both with each other and with the article.  The data does not contain any useful information to decide whether the excluded participant was participant n. 22 or 25, therefore I have calculated two Student t-tests (once with the exclusion of n. 22 and second time with the exclusion of n. 25).
Completed claim: single-trace
Claim evaluation
To test difference in intimacy between attention and no attention condition I have calculated Student t test for independent samples. Attention group (M = 4.58, SD = .816) had higher intimacy scores than the no attention group (M = 2.82, SD = .787), t(27) = 5.91, p = .000002695, d = 2.20. 
Single-trace claim
Coded claim 4 text (original paper): “Consistent with the hypothesis, intimacy was higher in the attention condition (4.58 ± .82) than in the no attention condition (2.82 ± .79), t(27) = 5.91, p &lt; .001, d = 2.20”

Discussion:  Reproduction outcome is dependent on the fact, whether I have successfully identified excluded participant. If different participant would be excluded, it would logically change the result. Thus, my analysis proved only the fact, that given the data provided by authors and excluding 1 of the two specific participants (assuming that just one of them was exactly the one excluded in the original study) it is possible to get the same results as provided in the article.
