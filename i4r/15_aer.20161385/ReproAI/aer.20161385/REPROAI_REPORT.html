Findings (for the authors)

P3 — low

ADV-001 — The reference-list dataset citation DOI does not resolve. The entry (paper_extracted.txt lines 2904–05) prints https://doi.org/10.1275/20161385 for “The Arrival of Fast Internet and Employment in Africa: Dataset.” 10.1275 is not an AEA handle and 20161385 alone is not a valid dataset suffix, so the citation as printed fails to point a reader to the files. The actual package is at 10.3886/E113156V1. Fix: cite the resolving replication DOI (10.3886/E113156V1), or the correct AER-native dataset handle, and verify it resolves. No effect on inference.

ADV-002 — One prose percentage uses a different baseline across samples. DHS is reported as “4.6 pp, or 6.9%”; re-derivation gives 0.046/0.68 = 6.76% under the printed Table 3 mean, and 6.87% only under the connected-baseline 0.67 (Table 1) — whereas the Afrobarometer “13.2%” (0.077/0.58) and SA “3.1%” (0.022/0.72) use the pooled mean. The base is not held constant across the three samples. Fix: state the conversion base, or uniformly use the mean-of-outcome (then DHS prints “6.8%”). No effect on inference.

ADV-003 — “Percent” vs “percentage point” used loosely for 0/1 coefficients. Table 3 Panel B “wants to work more” = −0.022 is described as reducing the probability “by 2.2 percent” (it is 2.2 pp); Table 5 prose calls DHS 0.044 / SA 0.014 “4.4 and 1.4 percent”. Coefficients are unambiguous in the tables. Fix: use “percentage points” for dummy-outcome coefficients; reserve “percent” for relative changes. No effect on inference.

What this package already does right

  • Replication policy compliance: full data + Stata code + GIS are deposited and linked from the AER article (openICPSR 10.3886/E113156V1) — this is the strongest element of the package.
  • Headline internal consistency: every pp/% conversion, baseline difference, t-sign, and cross-table N/mean check passes (40/41 ✅).
  • Transparent identification write-up: equation (1), connectivity radius (500 m), excluded (indirect) sample rules, SE clustering, and asinh transforms are all documented.
  • Thorough robustness battery (radius, grid-cell size, placebo roads/electricity/3G, leads/lags, permutation test, Conley spatial SEs, admin-unit clustering).

What this audit does not guarantee

  • No live data-level retabulation. The headline coefficients, clustered SEs, p-values, N and (absent) R² were not re-derived from raw microdata in this session: the binary replication files could not be downloaded past the openICPSR/Cloudflare gate from this shell, and Stata (the author tool) is not installed. This is an environment/access limitation, not evidence of a package defect. Recommended next step (preserved in advisory_plan.json): on an approved network, download 10.3886/E113156V1, run t1–t4.do, f4/f5/f7.do, fa1.do in Stata 14+, and diff all coefficients/SEs/N against Tables 3–9 and Table A1/A2.
  • Table 1 exact t-statistics and all regression standard errors require the microdata → not re-verified (± sign consistency only).
  • Table 5/6 fine-grained occupational/education cell-level signs were read from reconstructed tables; where the .txt extract dropped a minus, the PDF extraction was used, but a handful of minor cell signs were not exhaustively re-verified.
  • The WBES firm-level results are summarized from the online appendix and were not extracted/checked here.
  • The Fig. A1 ITU broadband-charge event-study and Figure 5 permutation-test estimates are figure-only and were not re-derived.
