Anomalous structure and dynamics in news
diffusion among heterogeneous individuals
Xiaochen Wang 1*, Yueheng Lan1,2 and Jinghua Xiao 1,2*
Previous research has suggested that well-connected nodes in a network (commonly referred to as hubs) are better at spreading
information than those with fewer connections (ordinary users). Here we investigate the roles of nodes with different numbers
of connections by studying how people share news online. Quantitative analysis shows that users without many connections
can sometimes spread news more effectively than well-connected users when the diffusion pattern has dendrite-like paths that
reach far into the network, leading to a non-Gaussian distance distribution. When the hubs dominate, however, the distribution
is Gaussian. Enhanced interactions among ordinary users are the key to the emergence of non-Gaussian characteristics. Finally,
we introduce a message-passing model that reproduces the observed diffusion features. This model shows that patterns domi-
nated by either hubs or ordinary users can be clearly demarcated by measuring the average number of forwards.

Study description We make a quantitative analysis about news diffusion on Sina Weibo and introduce a message-passing model simulating news spreading.
Research sample The research sample is the science-oriented news stories from the hot news website of Sina News Center. We collect the diffusion
processes of these news stories on Sina Weibo and the information of users include: nickname, sex, follower counts. The headlines of
news stories are collected from the ranking list of the number of shares in the science-oriented news bulletin board to get the news of
large diffusion size, which were posted in a period from January 2015 to June 2017. To avoid repeated headlines, the collection is done
every three days. Thus these news occur uniformly in the period and are representative. To study the social influence and avoid non-
universal personal factors (e.g., the user's social status and background affect the diffusion of military news, the user's preference or
aversion to the celebrities affect the diffusion of entertainment news), we choose the science-oriented news stories as major research
samples.
Sampling strategy The samples of news stories are collected from the ranking list of the number of shares in the science-oriented news bulletin board to get
news of large diffusion size, which were posted in a period from January 2015 to June 2017 and have a total of 9,120 pieces. To avoid
repeated headlines, the selection is done every three days.
Data collection We asked a commercial institution (https://www.shenjianshou.cn/) to help us collect data on Sina Weibo (http://weibo.com/). The
downloaded data includes all the posts of each piece of news (i.e. their respective user interactions and the follower counts of users) on
Sina Weibo which are publicly available (users with privacy restrictions are not included in the dataset).
Timing January 2015 to June 2017
Data exclusions The downloaded diffusion data from Sina Weibo consists of 2,205 news stories forwarded 634,800 times by 474,742 people. Some news
is passed by a vanishingly small number of individuals. As a result, only those of diffusion size (total number of times of users’ posting and
forwarding a news) greater than 100 are selected and we exclude 1,189 news stories whose diffusion sizes are too small. The data which
we study comprises 1,016 news stories forwarded 466,740 times by 355,453 people. To validate the selection in the main text, we also
studied the sets of the news of diffusion size greater than 50 or 150 to check the distribution of follower counts and find that the
threshold 100 gives similar distribution of follower counts and forwarding results as in the case with threshold 50 or 150.
Non-participation No experiments were conducted as part of this study.
Randomization No experiments were conducted as part of this study.
