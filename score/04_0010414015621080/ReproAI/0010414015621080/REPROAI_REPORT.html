Findings (for the authors)

P1 — high

ADV-001 — No replication data or code is obtainable for either headline analysis; only the internal arithmetic is verifiable. The paper's full text states no Dataverse/OSF/Data-and-Code link; footnote 21 offers only "available on request" for bootstraps. Automated searches of Harvard Dataverse (title, DOI, and author-keyword) returned no matching deposit, the DOI resolves to a bare citation, and the SAGE page is access-blocked. Consequently the Benin RCT regression coefficients and SEs (Table 2, C6–C7) and every Afrobarometer coefficient (Table 4, C18–C21), including the headline generalization (Vote b=0.04**), cannot be independently recomputed. Because the two primary analyses rest on undisclosed data, the standard of full reproducibility is not met. Fix (recommended): deposit a replication package (Benin respondent-level data + constructed Afrobarometer/DHS/EPR files + code + codebook, including the note-27 exclusion list) to a persistent repository and link it from the article DOI.

P3 — low

ADV-002 — "Marry into groups that are ... substantially larger (by 8 percentage points)" has an ambiguous referent. From Table 3 (country-round unit), comparing marital-status columns gives Spouse group size 0.317 (coethnic) vs 0.582 (cross-ethnic) = 26.5pp, yet the prose's "8pp" only reconciles with the within-cross-ethnic comparison of the leader's group (0.509) to the spouse's group (0.582) = 7.3pp. The sentence is understandable only if the reader infers the within-column comparison. Fix: state explicitly that in cross-ethnic marriages the leader marries into a group ~7–8pp larger than his own group.

What this audit does not guarantee

  • No headline regression was re-run. No raw data, replication package, or deposited code could be obtained for this 2016 article. R 4.6.1 was available and is the intended second-language reimplementation path, but with nothing to fit, Tables 2 and 4 are marked ➖ by necessity, not by choice.
  • Table 1 p-values were re-derived by pooled two-proportion z-tests on counts rounded from the published percentages; the authors may have used a Fisher-exact or chi-square test, so "✅" here means "consistent with a standard proportion test at the reported precision," not a byte-exact reproduction of their exact procedure.
  • Contextual background numbers (Benin's ~10M population; 2006 run-off "~75%") and cited external statistics (DHS rates, EPR group sizes, ELF/PREG indices) were treated as source citations, not part of the audited empirical pipeline, and were not independently re-derived from their external sources.
  • The online appendix tables (SI-3, SI-4, SI-5, SI-6, SI-8) were referenced in the text but were not included in the audit input; their contents could not be checked.
