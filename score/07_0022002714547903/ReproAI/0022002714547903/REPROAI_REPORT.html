Findings (for the authors)

P2 — medium

ADV-001 — No replication data or code exists for any headline model; primary statistics are unverifiable. The working-paper text references an "Online Appendix" with coding rules/codebooks but gives no URL (paper_extracted.txt lines 283, 775). A Harvard Dataverse title-exact search for this article returned 0 datasets; no OSF/GitHub replication link was found. All coefficients/SEs/p-values/N for the Weibull, OLS, and multinomial models (Tables 1–8) therefore cannot be independently reproduced and are marked ➖. Why it matters: the entire empirical core of the article is unreplicable from shipped or publicly archived materials. Fix: publish a complete replication package (both dataset builds TR and PRIO100 + analysis code) on Dataverse/OSF for the version of record, and cite it in the article; then re-run this audit against it.

ADV-002 — The audited artifact is the 2012 ICIP working paper, not the 2014 version of record for the stated DOI. paper_extracted.txt is the ICIP Working Paper 2012/5 preprint (41 pp.); the DOI resolves to Journal of Conflict Resolution 58(8):1390–1418 (2014). The tables/estimates audited here are a preprint snapshot that may differ from the published article. Why it matters: certifying a preprint does not certify the version of record that the DOI labels. Fix: re-run the audit against the final published PDF + online appendix and diff preprint vs published tables.

P3 — low

ADV-003 — PRIO100 severity sample inconsistency: Table 4 sums to 902 rows, Table 6 M1 has 913. The battledeaths-tabulated rows (122+757+23 = 902) are fewer than the OLS regression's M1 N (913), although the regression requires battledeaths data. Rechecked in R (01_internal_consistency.R). Fix: identify the 11 extra rows in Table 6 M1 or align the tabulated N.

ADV-004 — Footnote 12 missing-data percentages and scale are internally inconsistent. With 9 observed + 9 missing SNC wars the total is 18 and the missing fraction is 50.0%, yet the footnote reports 45% (which would need 20 SNC wars total); conventional is 28.0% vs a claimed 28.5%. The footnote's global mean 70,328.66 / max 2,097,705 (Vietnam) are on the PRIO100 per-conflict deaths scale but are reported against the TR section's 98 cases. Fix: recompute percentages against the correct per-technology totals and label the source scale explicitly.

ADV-005 — Table 1 (TR Weibull) observation counts are far above the war-level N and the panel structure is undocumented. Text states 142 ended wars (147 total in TR), yet Table 1 M1 has 1206 observations and M2–M4 have 899. A per-war Weibull AFT would be capped at 147; 1206 implies an undocumented conflict-year (spell) panel. Fix: state the unit of analysis and reconcile N with the war count.

What this audit does not guarantee

  • No source-level re-implementation was possible. No replication dataset or code was shipped or found (Dataverse/OSF), so none of the regression coefficients, SEs, p-values, or model Ns (Tables 1–8) was regenerated; they remain ➖. This is a limitation of the available materials, not a pass on their correctness.
  • The audit tests the preprint, not the version of record. Published JCR tables may differ; nothing here certifies the DOI-titled article's in-print numbers.
  • Directional/concluding claims (e.g., "irregular wars are won by incumbents", "irregular wars are the longest") were cross-checked against in-text signs/percentages and found internally consistent, but the underlying models were not rerun.
  • Cited background statistics (footnote 1's 66.34%/47.83%/26.09%/26.09%, drawn from Kalyvas & Balcells 2010) were not independently recomputed.
  • Figure 2 (Kaplan–Meier) and Figures 3–6 (outcome shares) embed values not printed in the text and were not verified pixel-/value-wise.
