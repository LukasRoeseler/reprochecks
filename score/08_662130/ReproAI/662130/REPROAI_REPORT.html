Findings (for the authors)

P1 — high

ADV-001 — No data or code is shipped or publicly deposited; the headline analyses are not independently recomputable. A full-text search of paper_extracted.txt finds no data-availability statement and no deposit link; the only "online appendix" mention (§III.B, p.159) is a translation of the TS instructions — not data. Web searches (DuckDuckGo, 2026-09-14) surface the published article (journals.uchicago.edu / JSTOR), the EconStor working-paper PDF, and IZA Discussion Paper 3835 (docs.iza.org/dp3835.pdf), but no ICPSR / Dataverse / OSF / GitHub / Zenodo data or code package for 10.1086/662130. Consequently the load-bearing empirical results — Table 2 treatment summaries, the key Mann–Whitney z=2.536, p=.011 underpinning Result 2, Levene's test (W=5.79, p=.018), Table 3 OLS, and Figures 1–2 — could not be recomputed from an independent raw source; 24 of 58 claims are marked ➖ for this reason. Why it matters: the audit can confirm the paper's internal logic is sound (every closed-form and conversion check passes) but cannot independently verify the data-generating process behind the excess-effort finding. Cause: 2012 publication predates replication-data mandates (JOLE/UCP did not require data posting then); no public replication package was deposited by the authors. Fix: deposit anonymized z-Tree experiment exports (or a tidy subject×stage CSV) plus a Stata .do/R script reproducing Table 2, Table 3, all nonparametric/tests, Levene, and Figures 1–2 on a persistent repository (IZA Data Archive, OSF, or Dataverse), and add a data-availability link to the article page (corresponding author: altmann@iza.org). This is the single step that would move all 24 ➖ claims to verifiable status.

P3 — low

ADV-002 — Small p-value deltas from coarse publication rounding (no inference impact). Recomputing one-sample t-tests from the 1-decimal-precision mean/SD and z→p from 2-decimal z yields: OS p=.543 vs printed .534 (Δ .009); OSL .204 vs .199 (Δ .005); TS st2 .569 vs .565; THS st3 .921 vs .918; OSL-vs-OS MW .347 vs .349 (Δ .002). Every affected test keeps the same accept/reject conclusion and apposite significance wording. Why it matters: none for conclusions; purely a precision artifact of reporting moments/z to coarse decimals, therefore not a defect. Fix: non-blocking; resolves automatically to sub-.0015 deltas once raw data are deposited (ADV-001).

What this audit does not guarantee

  • No independent raw-data reimplementation. The treatment means/medians/SD (Table 2), Mann–Whitney/Wilcoxon outcomes, Levene's test, Table 3 OLS coefficients/SEs/R², and Figures 1–2 histogram frequencies were not regenerated from the z-Tree dataset because that dataset and the analysis code were neither shipped nor publicly located. All the ✅/≈ verifications above are closed-form recomputes of the authors' own reported summary values (consistency audits), not independent reproductions from raw observations.
  • Theoretical-consistency of first-stage e*=74 is validated via the single-difference FOC and the Orrison et al. (2004) organizational-replication shortcut used by the authors; the full backward-induction continuation-value chain was not re-solved numerically, but it is algebraically equivalent by design (§II, §III.A).
  • OCR corruption: the scanned text occasionally smears equations/spellings; Tables 1–3 and all cited statistics used here were legible and cross-checked against the tabled column layouts.
  • Cited background figures from other studies (e.g. Orrison et al. 2004 avg effort 73.3; Burks et al. 2008 mean 2.4 / 25%) were not independently re-derived here.
  • One-shot design and $/€ conversions (i18.75 average earnings, i1 ≈ US$1.25 at time of experiment) are descriptive and not audited arithmetically beyond internal consistency.
